The ranking formula, and why no score is shown yet

SPEC.md 5.3 ranks on percentile ranks within a mode and window: 

score = 35*pct(RtD) + 25*pct(-MDD) + 25*pct(min t_j) + 15*pct(min(liveDays,90))
        - min(10, 100*breachRate)

Return-to-drawdown and annualised return need at least 30 days of marks, and this build has days, not months. So no score is computed and no desk is ranked. A number here would be a number about nothing.
